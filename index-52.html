01 — Positioning

Why Airplant Exists

Climate-control projects usually pass through four or five separate contractors — a duct installer, an electrician, a controls specialist, a pipefitter — each handing off to the next, each disclaiming the others' work.

Airplant exists to close that gap: one division, one point of accountability, across the entire mechanical, electrical, and controls scope of a climate system. The same technicians who install a system are the ones who commission it, service it, and answer the call when something goes wrong.

We're a Specialised Operating Division of Besbpo Group, a South African conglomerate spanning Built Environment, Real Estate, BPO, Logistics, Consultancy, and Security — which means Airplant draws on the same regulatory discipline, engineering rigour, and delivery standards already proven across the Group's other divisions.

Positioning Statement

Airplant designs, installs, and maintains complete climate systems — ducting, electrical, controls, and pipework — as one accountable scope of work.

Every Discipline, One Reference

Every plant needs power and protection. Airplant runs this scope directly, not through a sub-contractor.
